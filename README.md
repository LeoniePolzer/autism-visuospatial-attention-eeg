# Neural Oscillatory Dynamics During Visuospatial Attention in Autism

This repository contains MATLAB and R scripts used for EEG preprocessing, time-frequency decomposition, and statistical analyses in a study investigating neural oscillatory dynamics during visuospatial attention in autistic and neurotypical children.

## Study Overview
The study employed a modified Posner spatial cueing paradigm to investigate attentional orienting and reorienting. Spatial cues preceded targets appearing at either validly or invalidly cued locations.
EEG analyses focused on local oscillatory activity and functional connectivity in the alpha and beta frequency bands, examining how these dynamics varied by diagnostic group, cue validity, and spatial region.

## Repository Contents
The analysis pipeline comprises three main scripts:
- EEG preprocessing: EEG preprocessing, artifact detection and rejection, independent component analysis (ICA), and preparation of cleaned EEG datasets.
- TF decomposition:	Time-frequency decomposition and extraction of oscillatory power, inter-trial phase clustering (ITPC), power connectivity, and inter-site phase coherence (ISPC).
- Statistical analyses: Statistical analyses of behavioral and neural measures. Measures are analyzed to investigate group differences (ASD vs. NT), task-related modulation, relationships between neural measures, and associations with behavioral performance and clinical characteristics.
